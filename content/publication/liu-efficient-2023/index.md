abstract: To protect the integrity and reliability of images, we propose a watermarking-based
  approach for identifying whether the image has been tampered with during transmission
  and recovering the tampered content to its original form. The tampered pixel values
  are treated as unknowns in our proposed method and are calculated by solving a system
  of equations using Gauss-Jordan elimination. The recovery data is obtained by encoding
  the MSB matrix with a secret key and then embedded as part of the watermark for
  content self-recovery. The remainder of the watermark is authentication information
  for localizing the forged region, which is produced by MSB and recovery information
  of each block. Compared with existing works, the experimental results with our method
  show superior recovery capability.
